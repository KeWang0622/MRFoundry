# MRFoundry identity

The primary lockup pairs a blue MRI-bore symbol with **MR** in a heavy geometric
sans-serif and **Foundry** in a contrasting editorial serif. Light-background
and dark-background SVG variants are included. All letterforms are outlines:
there are no webfont requests or font fallback differences. Transparent canvas.

- `mrfoundry-logo-light.svg`: complete logo for white/light backgrounds.
- `mrfoundry-logo-dark.svg`: complete logo for dark backgrounds.
- `mrfoundry-wordmark-light.svg` / `-dark.svg`: typography without symbol.
- `mrfoundry-mark.svg`: compact MRI-bore mark / favicon.

Keep the approved scanner mascot as the brand's character. The bore mark provides
a small-size identifier where a detailed mascot is less legible. Preserve aspect
ratio, allow clearspace of at least one-quarter cap height, and do not redraw the
lettering in platform fonts. The light and dark files name their background use.

Typography: Avenir Next Heavy / Baskerville Bold converted to vector outlines.
Palette: cobalt #2876E8, deep ink #132536, dark-surface blue #82B7FF,
dark-surface lettering #EDF3FA. Body typography remains separate from the logo;
the paper retains its official MRM template fonts.

Design process: built-in OpenAI imagegen concept, followed by a native SVG rebuild
and manual spacing/contrast review. The exact concept prompt is in mrfoundry-logo-prompt.txt.
This is decorative identity artwork, not a scientific schematic.
